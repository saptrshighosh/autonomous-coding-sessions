# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free place for everyone who takes part, whatever their background, identity, or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Corrections should cite evidence.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give it constructively.
- Respect that people have different levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors to weaken a safety or honesty check.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, and contributions that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously do. Report a problem through GitHub's private reporting tools or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are handled with care and discretion.

This is a short statement written for this project. It applies in the repo, its issues and pull requests, and any space where someone represents the project.
